## 2. About the Band

### Long-form About Us

The Project DARBAR is a dynamic instrumental ensemble redefining the Indian fusion landscape. Rooted in the ancient tradition of Carnatic music and propelled by the energy of global sounds, the band weaves intricate raga-based melodies with modern grooves and harmonic textures, creating a soundscape that is both timeless and progressive.

At the heart of the group is the **violin**, carrying the soul of Carnatic music through rich raga explorations and virtuosic improvisations. **Drums** provide a rhythmic foundation that blends traditional tala cycles with elements of funk, progressive rock, and global percussion. The **piano** adds harmonic depth, bringing together Western classical voicings and Carnatic phrasing. The **guitar** bridges genres, moving between acoustic warmth, electric intensity, and ambient resonance, introducing rock, jazz, and fusion colours to the band's sound.

What distinguishes The Project DARBAR is its respect for classical integrity alongside a willingness to experiment with structure, rhythm, and arrangement. The band's compositions embrace complex time signatures, jugalbandi-style conversations between melody and rhythm, and immersive musical layers that showcase both precision and spontaneity.

The Project DARBAR is committed to bridging Indian classical and contemporary fusion music. Its performances and productions celebrate a vibrant cross-cultural dialogue, where the discipline of Carnatic tradition meets the expressive possibilities of modern music. Each piece is a celebration of innovation grounded in heritage, inviting listeners into a space where past and present flow as one.

*Source: pp. 3–4.*

### Koundinya Pannala — Violinist

**Short bio:**

Koundinya Pannala is the lead violinist and a driving force behind The Project DARBAR. Rooted in Carnatic tradition, his playing combines tonal clarity, expressive manodharma, and creative fusion, drawing on experience across more than 1,500 concerts.

**Full bio:**

Koundinya Pannala is the lead violinist and driving force behind The Project DARBAR, a band dedicated to reimagining and promoting contemporary Indian Carnatic classical music through innovative fusion. His passion lies in blending deeply rooted Carnatic traditions with modern and global musical influences, creating soundscapes that connect genres, cultures, and audiences.

Koundinya began learning the violin at the age of ten under **Vid. Sri Dr. Ashok Gurjale** and continues advanced training under **Vid. Sri MSN Murthy**. Having performed in **over 1,500 concerts**, he has developed a reputation as both a soloist and accompanist. His playing is grounded in Carnatic tradition, tonal clarity, expressive manodharma, and the ability to connect with diverse audiences.

His notable performances include:

- **Spirit of Youth**, The Music Academy, Chennai (2024).
- **World Trade Centre**, Mumbai, for an international conference (2016).
- **Rashtrapati Nilayam**, Hyderabad, during the President's visit (2022).
- **Indian Music and Dance Festival** (2025).
- **Nadaneerajanam**, Tirumala, for four consecutive years (2015–2018).
- Concerts at renowned organizations in Hyderabad and Chennai.
- International appearances at the **Hindu Temple of Greater Chicago** (Chicago), **Sri Venkateswara Lotus Temple** (California), and concerts organized by **Silicon Andhra** (California, USA).
- Corporate events for **Tech Mahindra**, the **Indian School of Business (ISB)** at its Hyderabad and Mohali campuses, and other organizations.

He earned a **Diploma in Carnatic Classical Music with Distinction** from **Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University** and is a **graded Carnatic violin artist with All India Radio**. Although he also holds a bachelor's degree in Information Technology, his passion led him to pursue music professionally.

His work spans fusion concerts, dance ballets, Harikathas, and film recordings. He has collaborated with composers including **M. M. Keeravani** and recorded violin for films including **Virata Parvam** (Suresh Productions) and **Rao Bahadur** (identified in the portfolio with Mahesh Babu/GMB Entertainment).

Through The Project DARBAR, Koundinya continues to explore new possibilities for Indian classical music while remaining grounded in its tradition.

*Source: pp. 9–11. Project names and film credits should be verified before publication.*

### Maanas Kanukollu — Pianist

**Short bio:**

Maanas Kanukollu is a pianist, guitarist, and music producer whose work brings together orchestral depth, Indian classical influences, synthesizers, and immersive sound design.

**Full bio:**

Maanas Kanukollu is a self-trained pianist and guitarist, music producer, and sound engineer who began his musical journey at the age of eleven. Fascinated by the emotional power of harmony and the possibilities of sound design, he developed a strong interest in synthesizers, orchestration, and cinematic soundscapes.

Driven to bridge classical music and emerging technology, Maanas pursued a **bachelor's degree in Sound Engineering** and, according to the portfolio, is studying toward a **diploma in Dolby Atmos music production and immersive sound mixing**. A professionally trained mixing and mastering engineer, he combines technical precision with artistic intuition to create immersive audio experiences.

He has performed live across India and contributed creatively to web series, short films, and cinematic projects. The portfolio identifies him as a **music programmer for the feature film “Decoit”** [title/spelling requires confirmation]. His work spans sound design, music programming, orchestration, and evocative composition for visual storytelling.

Maanas's musical voice combines orchestral depth, Indian classical essence, and contemporary synth work. Whether creating a cinematic score or contributing to a live fusion performance, he seeks to connect with audiences through rich, emotionally resonant sound.

*Source: pp. 12–13. The diploma is described as in progress in the source; do not change to completed without confirmation.*

### Chandra Mouli Mangudi — Guitarist

**Short bio:**

Chandra Mouli Mangudi is a guitarist, composer, and music producer with over eight years of experience, working across Carnatic fusion, pop, rock, jazz, R&B, and metal.

**Full bio:**

Chandra Mouli Mangudi is an Indian guitarist and music producer whose journey has been shaped by passion, self-motivation, and a musically rooted family background. With **over eight years of experience**, he has developed a versatile approach to **acoustic, electric, and bass guitar**, blending Carnatic and Western musical traditions.

His repertoire spans Carnatic classical fusion, pop, rock, jazz, contemporary R&B, and metal. As a composer and music programmer, he explores new creative directions and crafts his own intricate musical style.

Chandra Mouli also works as a **Music Facilitator with the NGO Enabling Leadership**, described in the portfolio as working in partnership with **Sony Music** to help underprivileged children develop leadership, confidence, teamwork, and responsibility through music.

A milestone in his journey was **June 20, 2015**, when he acquired his first acoustic guitar while pursuing his diploma studies. Through **The Project DARBAR**, he blends traditional Carnatic elements with modern influences, using music to connect cultures and audiences.

He also shares music through **Mouli Musical Creations** on platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. His creative path is defined by ongoing exploration, innovation, and an interest in music's emotional power.

*Source: pp. 14–15.*

### M. Meher Sri — Drummer & Percussionist

**Short bio:**

M. Meher Sri is a percussionist and music programmer with over 15 years of experience and more than 1,000 live performances. His work spans classical and contemporary rhythms, orchestration, and music education.

**Full bio:**

M. Meher Sri is a percussionist and music programmer with **over 15 years of experience** in the music industry. His expertise covers a wide range of instruments, including **tabla, mridangam, rhythm pads, drums, kanjeera, and African and Latin percussion**.

Having performed in **more than 1,000 live concerts** in India and internationally—including in **Germany, Sri Lanka, and Thailand**—he brings deep experience in classical and contemporary musical styles.

Meher Sri has contributed orchestration for television channels, **All India Radio**, and **Doordarshan**, and has spent **over a decade teaching musical instruments**. His recognitions and qualifications include a **Vadya Kalaimani Award**, a **mridangam diploma with distinction**, and **piano certifications from Trinity College London**.

Beyond live performance, he contributes through music arrangement, keyboard work, and rhythm programming for private albums and independent projects. In The Project DARBAR, he combines classical rhythmic depth with contemporary experimentation, helping shape the band's distinctive fusion sound.

*Source: pp. 16–17.*
